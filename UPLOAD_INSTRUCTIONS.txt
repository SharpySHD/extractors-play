What this patch changes:
- Mic remains persistent during gameplay (keeps v0.11.0 behaviour).
- "Hey Ricky" gets a short reply only: What? / What now? / Shoot. / Yeah? / Go on.
- Natural dart speech is accepted before the existing parser runs.
  Examples:
    1 20 1 19 1 18
    one twenty one nineteen one eighteen
    a 20 a 19 an 18
    give me a 20 a 19 an 18
    2 20 3 19 1 18
    20 19 18   (plain 12-20 board numbers default to singles for a 2-3 dart visit)
- Score is saved first, then Ricky calls the score.
- After the score call finishes, Ricky may add a short optional banter line.
- Cache/version bumped to v0.11.1 so browsers fetch the new scripts.
